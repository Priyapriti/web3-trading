
# Trader Behavior vs Market Sentiment Analysis

## 1. Objective
Analyze how trader behavior (profitability, leverage, volume, risk) aligns or diverges from market sentiment (Fear vs Greed).

## 2. Data Sources
- Historical Trader Data from Hyperliquid
- Fear & Greed Index

## 3. Methodology
- Cleaned and merged datasets by date
- Grouped trader metrics by sentiment
- Visualized trends using bar charts and line plots

## 4. Key Findings
- Traders are more profitable during Greed periods
- Leverage and risk-taking increase with positive sentiment
- Extreme Fear leads to volatile outcomes and cautious trading

## 5. Visuals
Include charts from your outputs folder:
- Fear & Greed Index over time
- Average PnL by sentiment
- Leverage and volume comparisons

## 6. Conclusion
Market sentiment strongly influences trader behavior. These insights can guide smarter trading strategies.
